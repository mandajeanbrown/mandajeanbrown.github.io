🛡️ Your First-Year Home Warranty
Your workmanship and materials coverage ends 12 months after closing... anything you want covered has to be reported before then.
No need to cram it into the last week. Five short checklists spread the walkthrough across your first year... check items off as you go, and your progress saves on this phone.
📖 Open the Warranty Manual
⚠️ SOME FIXES ARE ONE-TIME ONLY
Drywall cracks, cracked or loose tile, grout cracks, and caulking inside and out are repaired one time only during the first year. Unless something is leaking, note those items as you find them and save the repair for the Month 11 stage, so your one repair covers everything.
How to report a problem
1. Tell Adams Homes in writing as soon as you find a defect, and keep the date and the name of anyone you spoke with. A phone call alone does not count as a claim.
2. If the builder does not resolve it, the Maverick claim form must be received by Maverick no later than 30 days after your first-year coverage ends. Maverick: 727-308-2179 · maverickbuilders.com
3. Photograph every defect with the date visible, and do not repair it before Maverick has a chance to inspect it (emergencies excepted).
Systems coverage (electrical, plumbing, heating and cooling) runs 2 years from closing, and major structural coverage runs 10 years, so this page is worth keeping after your first year too.

📋 HOA Rules
A plain-language summary of the Kerns Ridge Declaration of Covenants, the document every owner agreed to at closing. If anything here ever differs, the recorded Declaration controls. The association is managed by CAMS.
🤔 Can I do this?
Type what you are thinking of doing and get the rule, plus what to do next.
Holiday lights: seasonal decorative lights are allowed from Thanksgiving to New Year's Day only. Christmas is the only holiday with a decoration window in the covenants.
Trash cans: cans may be out in the open only on the evening before pickup. The rest of the time they must be screened from view of the street and neighboring lots. Trash must be taken out at least once a week, and burning trash or debris is never allowed.
Temporary parking: an RV, boat, or trailer may sit on a lot, driveway, or street for up to 48 hours as a one-off (loading, a guest, a trip). Longer than that, it has to be stored elsewhere or in a closed garage.
Damage to your home: if your house is damaged, debris removal or rebuilding must begin within 60 days.
Fix-it window: if the association notifies you of a maintenance problem, you have 10 days to complete the repair (or start it, if it cannot be finished in 10 days) before the association can do it at your expense.
Dues: assessments not paid within 30 days of the due date accrue interest at 10% per year.
Any exterior construction, alteration, addition, or landscaping change needs written approval from the Architectural Review Committee (ARC) before work starts. That includes:
· Fences of any kind (uncoated chain link and barbed wire can never be approved)
· Any change to an exterior color, finish, or texture... house, roof, or fence
· Sheds and outbuildings (same materials as the house, behind the rear wall of the house)
· In-ground swimming pools, outside jacuzzis, and hot tubs
· Solar panels and other energy equipment
· Front-yard vegetable gardens, hammocks, statuary, and play equipment (a basketball goal beside the driveway is allowed)
· Dog houses, pens, and other animal shelters
· Exterior sculpture, fountains, flags, bird baths, and bird houses
· Signs of any kind (see the exceptions under Not Allowed)
· Window air-conditioning units (and never where visible from the front of the lot)
· Removing a tree over 4 inches thick (about 12½ inches around, measured 4½ feet up), except dead or diseased trees and trees within 10 feet of a house, driveway, sidewalk, drainage area, or septic field
· Above-ground pools... no exceptions
· Livestock, poultry, or exotic animals. Maximum 3 household pets per lot, no pets kept or bred for business, and pets must be leashed outside the house or a fenced area
· Overnight parking or storage of RVs, boats, trailers, campers, motor homes, commercial vehicles, motorcycles, or off-road vehicles on any street or lot, except inside a closed garage (the 48-hour exception is above). Passenger cars, SUVs, and light trucks without commercial markings may park in driveways
· Major or extended vehicle repairs on the lot. Vehicle assembly and messy hobbies only inside an enclosed garage
· Exterior clotheslines of any type
· Foil or other reflective material on windows
· Fuel tanks or water tanks visible from any street or lot
· Signs without ARC consent, except one professional For Sale or For Rent sign (max 4 sq ft), professional security signs, and legally required signs
· Exterior speakers, horns, sirens, bells, or amplifiers, except security devices
· Discharging firearms, including BB guns, pellet guns, bows, and sling shots. No target practice and no hunting anywhere in the subdivision (self-defense excepted)
· Short-term rentals. Leases must be at least 12 months
· A business with a sign, any outward evidence of activity, or customers coming to the home. A home office entirely inside the home is fine
· Blocking drainage, or placing anything in a drainage or utility easement that interferes with it
· Artificial vegetation or plastic animal decorations on the exterior (the covenants' example is pink flamingos)
· Leaving a garage door open when the garage is not in use, or shrinking a garage below 2-car size
· Individual mailboxes... mail is delivered to the centralized mailbox kiosks
· Maximum annual assessment: $900 per lot, rising no more than 10% per year without a member vote. New owners pay a $200 working capital contribution at closing
· Assessments are a lien on the lot. Late payments accrue interest at 10% per year after 30 days, and the association can sue or foreclose the lien and recover costs and attorneys' fees
· Fines follow the North Carolina Planned Community Act. The Declaration does not set a dollar amount
· After 10 days' written notice (emergencies excepted), the association may enter a lot to fix or remove a violation at the owner's expense. Unapproved construction can be stopped
Getting approval
Submit an Architectural Review Request Form through the CAMS Connect owner portal (portal.camsmgt.com) or the CAMS app. Send complete plans... what, where, size, materials, and colors. Incomplete requests are automatically denied. If the ARC does not approve or deny a complete application within 60 days, the request is deemed approved, but the work still has to follow the Declaration.
